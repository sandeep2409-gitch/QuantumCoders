About Quantum Coders

The Quantum Coders Club(QC) is an Student club in Pydah collge of Engineering (Autonomous) is an active organization aiming to foster a culture of innovation within our institution. TEAM QC provides platforms for creative students to learn, collaborate, and transform their innovative ideas into prototypes of viable products and services.

We promote technology-based startup ventures among college students and provide them with the necessary resources and support. We organize various workshops, webinars, Entalks, and competitions to help develop innovative ideas into successful entrepreneurial ventures.

Transforming ideas into innovation,
and innovation into success.
